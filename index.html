Before you get in

Cold water is hard on the body in the first seconds. The timer is a ceiling, not a target. Getting out early is always fine.

  • Do not plunge alone. Have someone within reach who can help you out.
  • Ease in. Go in feet first and keep your head above the water. A sudden gasp is a normal reaction to cold; breathe out slowly until it settles.
  • Get out right away if you go numb, shiver hard, cannot catch your breath, feel dizzy, confused or faint, or have chest pain.
  • Start short. If you are new to cold water, pick the shortest time. Colder water calls for less time, not more.
  • Not after alcohol, and not when you are ill or worn out.
  • Warm up slowly afterwards. Dry off, put on dry clothes and move around. Sit down if you feel lightheaded.

Not medical advice. This page is a timer. It does not know your health and makes no claims about what cold water does for you. Cold water raises breathing, heart rate and blood pressure quickly. If you have a heart or blood pressure condition, take medication for either, are pregnant, or are unsure, talk to your doctor before you try a cold plunge.

More on the risks: Cold Water Hazards and Safety from the U.S. National Weather Service, and The plunge into cold water comes with risks from American Heart Association News.

How the timer works

  • 30 seconds to 5 minutes. The timer does not go longer. That limit is a cautious choice for a general tool, not a goal to work up to.
  • The countdown keeps true time. It reads the clock rather than counting ticks, so it stays right if the phone lags or the tab was in the background.
  • Sound needs one tap. Browsers only allow sound after you tap Start. On an iPhone the ring/silent switch mutes web audio.
  • "I'm out" stops the clock and logs the time you were actually in. Under 5 seconds counts as a false start and is not logged.
  • A session is the plunges since you opened the page or pressed "Start a new session".
  • The log lives in your browser (local storage), up to the last 200 plunges. Clearing your browser data clears it.
